Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This policy explains what information RP Delivery Service collects, why, and your choices.

1. Information we collect

  • Account: name, phone number, email and password (the password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication, and we cannot see it).
  • Orders: delivery address, items ordered, amount, payment method, order status and time.
  • Partners: business name, address, menu or products, and details needed for verification.
  • Riders: name, phone, and details needed for verification, plus delivery history and earnings.
  • Technical: basic device and browser information needed to run the app securely.

2. How we use it

  • To process and deliver your orders and show order tracking.
  • To verify Partners and Riders and keep the platform safe.
  • To give support, handle complaints and refunds.
  • To improve the app and follow legal requirements.

3. Who sees your information

  • The Partner you order from sees your name, phone, address and items, to prepare and hand over your order.
  • The Rider delivering your order sees your name, phone and address, only for that delivery.
  • RP admins can see account and order data to run the service and solve problems.
  • Google Firebase stores our data on its servers (our database region is Mumbai, India) and acts as our service provider.
  • We may share information with authorities when the law requires it.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Medicines and health information

Orders for health products and medicines may reveal health-related information. We use it only to complete your order. If a prescription is required, it will be used only for verification by the pharmacist.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your information while your account is active and for as long as needed for orders, accounts, complaints and legal reasons. After that we delete it or remove identifying details.

6. Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your information, or to close your account, by contacting us below. We may keep some records where the law requires.

7. Security

We use trusted services and access rules so people can see only the data they need. No system is 100 percent secure, so please keep your password private and never share your OTP except to the Rider when receiving your order.

8. Children

RP is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available in the app.
