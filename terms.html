1. What RP is

RP Delivery Service is a local platform that connects customers with nearby restaurants, food shops, pharmacies and health-essential stores ("Partners"), and with delivery riders ("Riders"). RP provides the technology. Food and products are prepared and sold by the Partners, and delivery is done by Riders.

2. Who can use it

You must be 18 or older and give correct information (name, phone, address). You are responsible for your account and password. Tell us immediately if you think someone else is using your account.

3. Orders and prices

  • Prices, delivery fee and service fee are shown in the app before you confirm an order.
  • Delivery times are estimates and can change because of traffic, weather or store workload.
  • A Partner may reject an order, for example when an item is unavailable or the store is closed.

4. Payment

Currently you can pay cash or UPI on delivery. Please keep the exact amount ready. Other payment methods may be added later.

5. Delivery and OTP

For your safety, a 4-digit delivery OTP is shown in your order. Share it with the Rider only when you have received your order. Please be reachable on your phone and give a correct address. If we cannot reach you, the order may be cancelled and charged as per the Partner's policy.

6. Cancellations, problems and refunds

  • You can ask to cancel before the Partner starts preparing the order.
  • If your order is wrong, missing items or in bad condition, contact us within 24 hours with details (and a photo if possible).
  • We review each case and may offer a refund or replacement. Decisions are made fairly case by case.

7. Medicines and health products

  • Prescription-only and restricted medicines cannot be bought directly in the app. They need a valid prescription and verification by a registered pharmacist, as required by law.
  • Over-the-counter and general health items are sold by licensed pharmacies or stores. Product information is for general help and is not medical advice. For health problems, please consult a doctor or pharmacist.
  • We or the Partner may refuse or cancel any medicine order that does not meet legal requirements.

8. Food safety

Food Partners are responsible for food quality, hygiene and their food licence (such as FSSAI). Please tell us about allergies directly to the store, as we cannot guarantee allergen information.
